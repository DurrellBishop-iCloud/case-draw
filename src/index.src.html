Settings

Phone

Drawing sits

Anything drawn outside the case outline, and touching it, becomes a solid piece in that colour: flush with the back and going towards the screen as deep as the Beyond slider in the 3D view says (from 1 mm, the panel alone, to the whole depth of the case). Pen strokes and Beck shapes only: photos and text stay inside the case. Loose shapes that don't touch the case are left out. Button holes and ports are cut through the pieces.

Case sides

Cycle through:

Magnet ring

A channel for a stick-on metal ring (MagSafe size), with plastic lips inside and outside it. Its top comes flush with the highest thing on the back, and the drawing keeps out of it. The surround prints as its own part, so it can have its own colour.

Colours

The panel and case share one colour; with the three inks that's the four tools on the U1. Tap a selected ink in the toolbar to change its colour.

Phone and case dimensions mm

Back view: x from the left edge, y from the top edge. A cutout is a circle (x, y, d) or a rounded rectangle (x, y = centre, w, h, r), like the camera island. cutoutClearance is added all round. Side cutouts run along an edge, measured from the top-left corner. Anything marked placeholder is a guess, so measure and correct it, or drag camera cutouts with the Cutouts tool.

inkMargin keeps strokes clear of the plate edge and the cutouts, so raised ink never sits flush with an edge where it could catch.

Case: one piece, printed back-down. A back slab (slabThickness) with the wall (frameWall) rising from it and a lip over the screen (lipDepth, lipThickness; lipDrop lowers it below the screen face for phones whose edges curve away, e.g. 0.7 on iPhone 17; lipRaise makes it thicker, so it stands proud of the glass). Button holes are rounded slots in the wall: from/to along the edge from the top-left corner, fromScreen = centre below the screen face, height; buttonClearance is added at each end and buttonChamfer bevels the outside edge. cutoutInset brings the camera opening in that far on every side; cutoutEdge keeps it that far in from the edge of the phone. A side cutout without fromScreen runs the full wall height with windowRadius corners.

Panel: a flat plate the size of the case back (panelThickness), glued to the slab. In inlay mode it carries short collars round the camera holes (collars, collarWall, collarClearance) that key into oversized holes in the slab, so it can only go on straight.

Files

The test coupon is the top-left 32 mm corner of the case and of the panel, side by side: press the phone's corner in to check the lip and clearance, and drop the panel corner on to check the camera collar. Print it before the whole case.

The case 3MF is oriented back-down, ready to slice. The 3MF opens in Snapmaker Orca or Bambu Studio with every part already on a filament slot: the panel colour on 1, then Ink 1, 2 and 3, then the case colour (a fifth colour shares the nearest slot). Load matching filaments in those slots; the slots each colour went to are shown here after you save. Where inks overlap, the stroke drawn last wins, as on screen.
