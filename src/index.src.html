Back view: x from the left edge, y from the top edge. A cutout is a circle (x, y, d) or a rounded rectangle (x, y = centre, w, h, r), like the camera island. cutoutClearance is added all round. Side cutouts run along an edge, measured from the top-left corner. Anything marked placeholder is a guess, so measure and correct it, or drag camera cutouts with the Cutouts tool.

inkMargin keeps strokes clear of the plate edge and the cutouts, so raised ink never sits flush with an edge where it could catch.

Case: one piece, printed back-down. A back slab (slabThickness) with the wall (frameWall) rising from it and a lip over the screen (lipDepth, lipThickness). Button openings are windows with windowRadius corners.

Panel: a flat plate the size of the case back (panelThickness), glued to the slab. In inlay mode it carries short collars round the camera holes (collars, collarWall, collarClearance) that key into oversized holes in the slab, so it can only go on straight.

Files

The test coupon is the top-left 32 mm corner of the case and of the panel, side by side: press the phone's corner in to check the lip and clearance, and drop the panel corner on to check the camera collar. Print it before the whole case.

The case 3MF is oriented back-down, ready to slice. The panel 3MF opens in Snapmaker Orca or Bambu Studio as one object with a Panel part and one part per ink. Right-click each part to assign its filament. Where inks overlap, the stroke drawn last wins, as on screen.
